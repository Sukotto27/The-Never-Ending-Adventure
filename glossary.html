The continent of Estranta is home to ten distinct kingdoms with unique cultures, climates, and mysteries.

Kingdom of Ardrenhold
Human ruled · Western coast · Lush, green, forest-rich
Known for: Brave and valiant knights, welcoming taverns.
Capital: Ardrenkeep — a fortified city housing the main keep and throne of the noble king.
Cities: Ealdenford, Wulfhelm, Aethelstow, Grimscastel.
Villages: Kinsburg, Hearthmere, Wyrmwood, Brigstow, Eorlingstead, Alderwood.
Notable Locations: Raven's Perch Lookout (wyvern nesting grounds nearby), The Shadow Cavern (legendary Raven Knight armor — solve his riddle to claim it), Ardrenwood (goblin tribe), The Goldenfields (annual Harvest Festival), The Ashbound Altar & The Oaken Barrow (ancient artifact fragments).
Kingdom of Dwynbroch
Human ruled (with Dwarves) · Northwestern region · Mountainous, pine forests, bogs
Known for: Lively music and art, fascination with dragons.
Capital: Aberwyn — a picturesque city at a river mouth.
Cities: Lochvale, Morvarth, Glencairn, Glenvarloch.
Villages: Dalmore, Thornwood, Braefern, Dunleith, Glenmire.
Notable Locations: Lochvale Arena, The Thornwood Forest (enchanted, shifting paths; reclusive dragon and giant spiders within), The Misty Moors, The Dragon's Spine Ridge (dragon egg hidden here), The Weeping Waterfall (treasure cave behind the falls), The Cairn of Dunan (ancient artifact fragment).
Kingdom of Brythwen
Human ruled · Southern coast · Coastal
Known for: Continental hub for traders and merchants; largest marketplace on the continent.
Capital: Brythford.
Cities: Eldenbrook, Valdara, Greystone, Windhall.
Villages: Thornwick, Brambleton, Oakshire, Riverwell, Meadowford, Drunmar (quarantined — mysterious illness).
Notable Locations: Sapphire Harbor, The Shadow Market (illegal goods; gangs and pirates), Glimwood Castle (abandoned; dark secret), Stormwatch Tower, The Wyvern's Windspire, The Ruins of Windwhisper (wind chime points to hidden treasure), The Shattered Citadel of Caldris (ancient artifact fragment).
Kingdom of Nithrond
Elf ruled · Southeastern region · Forested, autumnal
Known for: Elven prowess in archery; the Grand Archives — largest library in Estranta (access requires Elven Loremasters Society membership).
Capital: Thalorion — buildings carved from shimmering white stone, grown from enchanted roots.
Cities: Sylvaen, Virelysar, Caeleth, Namorelith.
Villages: Elmyralen, Firaelis, Tirwynn Hollow, Liraen's Cross, Thandelmere.
Notable Locations: The Grand Archives, Rendrid's Moonlit Bazaar (open only under the full moon), The Moonlit Glade (ethereal tree reveals underground chamber on full moon), The Starfall Burrow (rare Starfall Gem), The Veil of Vines (ancient artifact fragment).
Kingdom of Sivanrift
Elf ruled (with Humans) · Southwestern region · Gardens, canyon networks, forest
Known for: Rare plants grown nowhere else in the world; the Grand Gardens in the capital.
Capital: Myrthill — silverwood towers and marble courtyards; a symbol of inter-racial harmony.
Cities: Aurelwyn, Talandras, Velendar, Iriandel.
Villages: Willowfen, Duskwatch, Elowen's Field, Halorin Cross, Rivensong.
Notable Locations: The Grand Gardens of Sivanrift, The Cursed Forest (Ithalia's curse; begins quest "The Sword of Ithalia"), The Echoing Canyons, The Twilight Vineyards, The Thornward Pillars (ancient artifact fragment).
Kingdom of Naradreth
Elf ruled · Eastern coast · Coastal
Known for: Hostile to strangers, especially non-elves; on the brink of civil war between the Twelve Great Houses.
Capital: Velra'syl — divided into twelve noble districts; outsiders are rarely welcome.
Cities: Ilryndor (naval academy; Silent Guard), Draes'ythil (all communication via telepathic glyphs — no one speaks aloud).
Villages: Tiryll's Hollow, Mythren's Gate, Vaerlyn Reach.
Notable Locations: Sunspire Tower, The Singing Springs, Twilight Mirror Lake (submerged elven shrine), Eclipse Grove, The Sunken Shrine of Vaeryn (ancient artifact fragment).
Kingdom of Feldarún
Dwarf ruled · Central mountains · Hidden in mountainous terrain
Known for: Mining heritage, master craftsmanship; hostile to strangers and non-dwarves.
Capital: Khúralgron — a grand forge-city carved into a volcanic caldera.
Cities: Darakthul, Brondrak Kazel, Vurndrim, Tharnhold.
Villages: Stonebraith, Molgrin's Deep, Emberstead, Grummveir, Kaerbryn Hollow.
Notable Locations: The Murkridge Hollows (goblins, cave trolls; great riches within), The Ironbell Forge, The Ironback Mountains (trolls and giants), The Emberheart Tavern, The Irondeep Catacombs (ancient artifact fragment).
Kingdom of Wistravael
Dwarf ruled · Northern region · Mountainous, forested
Known for: Dwarven architecture, connection to earth and heavens.
Capital: Durkendal — built beneath the Mountain of Stars; vast underground observatory and council halls.
Cities: Tharnvyr, Vordrun Keep, Stonewake, Kragmire Hold.
Villages: Flinthollow, Silverdeep, Bramgar's Rest, Hearthglen, Wyrshade.
Notable Locations: The Dwarven King's Keep, The Vordynmire Caverns, The Gilded Anvil, The Drakeling Pass (young but fierce dragons), The Dreaming Cavern (dream crystal grants visions of future events), The Celestial Observatory (star metal location revealed), The Starfall Cradle (ancient artifact fragment).
Kingdom of Orindroth
Elf ruled · Central region · Ancient, dense forest — the entire kingdom lies within the trees
Known for: Sacred trees with mystical properties; forest shrouded in mystery, easy to become lost in.
Capital: Elarien — built within a ring of ancient towering trees; haven for druids and forest sages.
Cities: Valorin (canopy city woven into treetops), Ilietha, Tirelyn Reach, Norowynn's Veil (veiled grove; meeting place of the Circle of Eldertrees).
Villages: Larethwyn, Feyran's Nest, Thiriel Glen, Mireth's Bloom, Wendralis Shade.
Notable Locations: The Wandering Wyvern Inn, The Eldertree Circle, The Moonstone Hollow (elven cloak of camouflage), The Eldertree Threshold (ancient artifact fragment).
Kingdom of Rendarost
Dwarf ruled · Far northern region · Harsh, snowy, arctic
Known for: Hardiness and resilience; The Dwarven Archives (open to the public); frost ale brewed by a secret method.
Capital: Kragmoor — tunneled into a frozen cliff face; frost-forged weapons sold only to kin.
Cities: Dravundar, Iskroldir, Thuldrenheim, Varkol's Crown.
Villages: (far northern outposts).
Notable Locations: The Dwarven Archives of Rendarost, Healing Hot Springs, The Frost Troll Ridge (frost giants, frost trolls), The Frostflower Fields (ice wyrms beneath), The Frostfire Chasm (frozen flame artifact), The Frostbound Sanctum & The Duskstone Vault (ancient artifact fragments).

Skill Tiers

Each skill progresses through seven tiers as it levels up:

  • Novice (level 1) — starting rank
  • Apprentice (level 10)
  • Journeyman (level 25)
  • Adept (level 50)
  • Expert (level 75)
  • Master (level 90)
  • Grandmaster (level 99) — pinnacle rank

Skill Tier Titles

Each skill generates its own progression title in the form The [Tier] [Noun] — e.g. The Journeyman Hunter, The Master Smith, The Grandmaster Shadow. The title's bonus equals the skill-tier bonus at that level.

  • Novice (lv 1) — no title awarded
  • Apprentice (lv 10) — +1 to that skill
  • Journeyman (lv 25) — +1 to that skill
  • Adept (lv 50) — +2 to that skill
  • Expert (lv 75) — +2 to that skill
  • Master (lv 90) — +3 to that skill
  • Grandmaster (lv 99) — +4 to that skill · triggers Master's Guild encounter

Kingdom Stats

Each kingdom tracks six living stats that drift over time based on player actions and world events.

  • Prosperity (0–100) — economic health; affects merchant stock and prices
  • Happiness (0–100) — tracks toward (Prosperity×0.6 + Stability×0.4); NPC disposition baseline
  • Stability (0–100) — law and order; high crime erodes it over time
  • Military (0–100) — defensive strength; affects random threat events
  • Crime (0–100) — bandit activity, theft frequency; high crime drains stability
  • Trade (0–100) — merchant variety and caravan frequency
  • Population (thousands) — falls when prosperity collapses for extended periods

Kingdom condition thresholds (avg of Prosperity + Happiness + Stability): Thriving ≥78 · Stable ≥62 · Struggling ≥46 · Declining ≥28 · Collapsing below 28 · Fallen = 0/0/0

World Economy Events

Dynamic events that alter NPC dialog, merchant pricing, and travel danger for a set number of time ticks. Rumors about active events may surface in tavern conversations.

  • Civil War — roads unsafe; weapon prices spike
  • Kingdom at War — recruitment active; supply prices high
  • Plague — healers overwhelmed; potion prices spike
  • Famine — food scarce and expensive
  • Prosperity — goods plentiful; prices down
  • Trade Boom — merchant variety high; prices down
  • Bandit Surge — roads dangerous; escort demand up
  • Harvest Festival — food and ale near-free
  • Magic Shortage — alchemical ingredients rationed; potion prices spike
  • Dragon Threat — weapons in demand; settlements on edge
  • Mining Boom — metal goods cheap; smiths flood the area
NPC Dialog & Disposition

Conversations are contextual, not scripted. They can be affected by many factors, such as trait compatibility, reputation, relation to NPCs, world events, and more.

  • Terrorize — morality −6 · kingdom rep −4 · aggressive
  • Malicious — morality −2 · kingdom rep −2 · aggressive
  • Malevolent — morality −3 · kingdom rep −2 · aggressive
  • Neutral — no shift
  • Valiant — morality +2 · kingdom rep +1 · heroic
  • Heroic — morality +3 · kingdom rep +2 · heroic
  • Inspired — morality +5 · kingdom rep +3 · heroic

Trait affinity: each shared trait with a revealed NPC trait = +1; each opposing trait = −1. Capped ±3. Mismatched tones vs. NPC morality add an extra −1 clash penalty.

d20 Roll System

All action rolls use a d20. The base roll is modified by: skill level bonus, condition modifiers, active title bonus, camp bonus, equipment wear, weather severity, spirit, trait roll bonuses, and situational modifiers. The adjusted total is capped at 20 and classified into one of five tiers.

  • Tier 1 (1–5) — Critical Failure · large negative outcome · hope −2
  • Tier 2 (6–10) — Failure · negative outcome · hope −1
  • Tier 3 (11–14) — Partial Success · middling outcome · no hope change
  • Tier 4 (15–18) — Success · good outcome · hope +1
  • Tier 5 (19–20) — Critical Success · exceptional outcome · hope +2
Weather

Weather is dynamic and region-dependent, and has an effect on travel.

  • Severity 0 — Sunny, Clear, Partly Cloudy (no penalty)
  • Severity 1 — Cloudy, Overcast, Fog, Mist, Windy, Drizzle
  • Severity 2 — Strong Wind, Light Rain, Rainy, Light Snow (+30% travel time)
  • Severity 3 — Heavy Rain, Snowy (+60% travel time)
  • Severity 4 — Stormy (dangerous travel)
  • Severity 5 — Blizzard (near-impassable)
Time of Day

  • 🌤️ Afternoon · ⛅ Mid-Afternoon · 🌇 Late Afternoon · 🌆 Evening
  • 🌃 Mid-Evening · 🌙 Dusk · 🌑 Night · ⭐ Late Night
Item Wear & Condition

Weapons and armor have a wear percentage, degrading with use over time. Wear affects roll bonuses for weapon skills.

  • 100–75% — Excellent (+3 modifier to associated rolls)
  • 74–50% — Good (+1)
  • 49–25% — Fair (±0)
  • 24–10% — Worn (−1)
  • 9–1% — Damaged (−2)
  • 0% — Broken (unusable)

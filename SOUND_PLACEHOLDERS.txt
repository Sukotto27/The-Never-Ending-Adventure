SOUND PLACEHOLDERS
==================
These sound files are registered in sound.js and already wired in the game.
Drop the audio files into the folders listed below and they will play automatically.
All files should be MP3.


── AMBIENCE ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/ambience/

  ambience_coastal.mp3
    Plays in Coastal and Ocean biomes (day or night).
    Suggest: ocean waves, distant seagulls, wind off water.

  ambience_mountain.mp3
    Plays in Mountain / Mountains biomes during the day.
    At night falls back to ambience_common_night.
    Suggest: high-altitude wind, distant eagle cry, sparse birdsong.

  ambience_river.mp3
    Plays in River and Lake biomes (day or night).
    Suggest: flowing water, frogs, light insect hum.

  ambience_town_night.mp3
    Plays in City / CapitalCity / Village zones at night.
    Currently falls back to ambience_common_night.
    Suggest: distant tavern chatter, night watchman bell, occasional dog bark.

  ambience_mine.mp3  [REGISTERED — wire trigger when mine zone/location is added]
    Plays inside underground mine locations.
    Suggest: deep cave drip, distant pickaxe echoes, low stone reverb.


── SOUND EFFECTS ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/effects/

  quest_update.mp3
    Plays when a quest objective is ticked off (✅ message).
    Suggest: short ascending chime or parchment-rustle.

  quest_complete.mp3
    Plays when a full quest is completed (🏆 message).
    Suggest: triumphant fanfare sting, 1–2 seconds.


── COMBAT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/actions/

  sword_swing.mp3
    Plays on every player melee/ranged attack.
    Suggest: a sharp whoosh-and-impact; something between a sword swipe and a
    dull thud. Should be short (under 0.8s) so it doesn't overlap enemy counter.

  shield_block.mp3
    Plays when the player takes the Defend stance.
    Suggest: heavy clank of a raised shield, or a grunt and armour scrape.

  combat_flee.mp3
    Plays when the player attempts to flee combat (both success and failure).
    Suggest: quick scrambling footsteps, urgent thud of boots on dirt.

  combat_victory.mp3
    Plays when combat ends in player victory (enemy HP reaches 0).
    Suggest: short triumphant sting — a brief sword-sheathe plus an upward
    musical phrase. Keep under 2s.

  combat_defeat.mp3
    Plays when the player's HP reaches 0 and the defeat sequence begins.
    Suggest: low reverberant toll, like a slow bell or heavy gong fading out.


── MAGIC / SPELLS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/actions/

  spell_fire.mp3
    Plays when casting Fire Bolt or Lightning in combat.
    Suggest: sharp crackle and whoosh — fire igniting with energy.
    Lightning variant can be the same file; if you want two, name the second
    spell_lightning.mp3 and register it separately.

  spell_dark.mp3
    Plays when casting Dark Bolt, Wither, or Life Drain.
    Suggest: low, eerie resonance — a wisp of dark energy releasing.

  spell_heal.mp3
    Plays when casting Mend (in or out of combat).
    Suggest: soft ascending shimmer or choir swell — soothing, brief.

  spell_meditate.mp3
    Plays when starting the Meditate action (mana restoration).
    Suggest: a single soft singing bowl strike fading slowly.

  spell_ward.mp3
    Plays when a protective ward is woven (Ward spell success).
    Suggest: a crystalline ping or bubble-formation sound.

  spell_dark_sight.mp3
    Plays when Dark Sight is cast (shadow whisper / map reveal).
    Suggest: a low breath or susurrus — like darkness exhaling.

  blood_ritual.mp3
    Plays when a Blood Magic Ritual begins.
    Suggest: slow heartbeat pulse, slightly unsettling.


── FISHING ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/actions/

  fish_bite.mp3
    Plays at the "Something on the line — HOOK IT!" moment.
    Suggest: a short line-tension twang or quiet water plop. Must be instantly
    recognisable so the player knows to act fast.

  fish_catch.mp3
    Plays when the player successfully hooks and reels in a fish.
    Suggest: a wet splash and quick reel-click, triumphant but brief.


── HUNTING ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/actions/

  hunt_kill.mp3
    Plays on a successful clean kill or final arrow hit.
    Suggest: distant thud of an animal falling through undergrowth — natural,
    not gory. Keep it short and muted.


── SOCIAL / TOWN ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/effects/

  crowd_cheer.mp3
    Plays on a busking or local-games high-tier success.
    Suggest: small crowd applause and a cheer — maybe 1–2 seconds. Warm and
    lively, not stadium-scale.

  pickpocket_success.mp3
    Plays when a pickpocket attempt succeeds (Thievery tier ≥ 4).
    Suggest: very quiet coin jingle — subtle, conspiratorial.

  pickpocket_fail.mp3
    Plays when a pickpocket attempt is detected (tier 1–2).
    Suggest: sharp gasp or a short "hey!" sting — brief social alarm.


── ALCHEMY / COOKING ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/actions/

  brew_potion.mp3
    Plays during the Brew / Quick Brew action while the potion completes.
    Suggest: liquid bubbling in a glass vessel, gentle and rhythmic.

  cooking_sizzle.mp3
    Plays when food is placed on the campfire to cook.
    Suggest: a satisfying meat-or-fish sizzle on a hot surface.


── EXPLORATION / TRAVEL ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/effects/

  discovery.mp3
    Plays when a new map location is discovered for the first time.
    Suggest: a quiet ascending chime or distant bell — a sense of wonder, not
    a full fanfare (save that for quest_complete).

  sail.mp3
    Plays when a sailing / sea travel action begins.
    Suggest: wind filling a sail, gentle creaking of wood, water rushing.


── UI / FEEDBACK ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Folder: sounds/ui/

  critical_success.mp3
    Plays on a natural 19–20 roll (tier 5 / critical success).
    Suggest: a bright, punchy musical hit — sharper and shorter than
    level_up. Something like a coin-flip-and-chime.

  critical_fail.mp3
    Plays on a natural 1–5 roll (tier 1 / critical failure).
    Suggest: a low comedic or dramatic thud — a muted horn or dropped-
    shield clang. Should feel weighty but not devastating.
